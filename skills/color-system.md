# Color as a semantic system
Derive a palette from the subject, audience and requested tone rather than forcing a fixed theme.
- Use --wpx-* background, foreground, muted, accent, secondary accent and divider tokens.
- Assign colors to chapter identity, emphasis and data categories; preserve their meaning across pages.
- Check actual text/background pairs; body text generally needs 4.5:1 contrast. Never encode meaning through color alone.
- Use neutral space and saturation to support hierarchy. Flat backgrounds are valid; gradients, texture and shadow must serve the design.
- Inspect small labels, translucent layers, charts and navigation states at presentation size. Consider projectors and color-vision differences.
- Capture the rendered page and correct contrast locally; do not recolor unrelated content to fix one defect.
